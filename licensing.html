Licensing & Booking

Produce
this play

Play It Straight is available for production by community colleges, colleges, universities, and community theaters.

01 — Royalty
$100 per performance · flat rate · no minimum
02 — How It Works

Booking process.

  1. Submit your inquiry through the contact form
  2. Include your organization name and anticipated performance dates
  3. Receive direct follow-up on licensing approval and performance rights
Request Licensing Information →

FAQ.

  • Who can produce this play? Community colleges, colleges, universities, and community theaters.
  • Is this black box friendly? Yes. Staging is flexible with manageable technical requirements.
  • How do we request rights? Contact the playwright directly using the site contact form.
  • Is there a minimum run? No minimum performance requirement is specified.
03 — A Note

Producing
responsibly.

Organizations are encouraged to engage thoughtfully with the play's themes, which include free expression, identity, and the role of the arts in public education.

Production approval is handled directly and collaboratively to support organizations in presenting the work responsibly and successfully.
