Licensing & Booking

Produce
this play

Play It Straight is recommended for professional theatre companies, community theatres, college-level theatre programs, and courageous high school drama departments.  It works exceedingly well for professional theatre companies that have an educational/ youth component. 

01 — How It Works

Booking process.

  1. Submit your inquiry through the contact form
  2. Include your organization name and anticipated performance dates
  3. Receive direct follow-up on licensing approval and performance rights
Request Licensing Information →

FAQ.

  • Is this black box friendly? Yes. Staging is flexible with manageable technical requirements.
  • How do we request rights? No agent, no publisher, no intermediary of any kind — you can deal directly with the playwrights. Just use the website contact form to reach out to us and we'll respond in a timely manner.
  • Is there a minimum run? No minimum performance requirement is specified.

03 — A Note

Producing
responsibly.

Organizations are encouraged to engage thoughtfully with the play's themes, which include free expression, identity, and the role of the arts in public education.

Production approval is handled directly and collaboratively to support organizations in presenting the work responsibly and successfully.

04 — Content Advisory

Know before
you produce.

This play contains strong language, homophobic slurs, discussion of conversion therapy, political extremism themes, accusations of misconduct, and mature discussions of sexuality and identity.

Organizations are encouraged to engage thoughtfully with the play's themes. Production approval is handled directly and collaboratively to support organizations in presenting the work responsibly and successfully.

We encourage the use of Play It Straight to support organizations which support members of the LGBTQ+ community.
