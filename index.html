PLAY IT STRAIGHT

A sarcastic remark during a high school drama rehearsal is taken out of context and sparks a parent complaint, triggering a formal inquiry that exposes tensions around censorship, LGBTQ identity, artistic expression, and parental authority within the public school system.

Set in a public high school, PLAY IT STRAIGHT follows students and faculty after a classroom comment escalates into a legal and political conflict. As the school turns toward institutional self-protection, students are forced to navigate identity, fear, and the pressure to stay silent.
