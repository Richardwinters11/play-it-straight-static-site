LGBTQ+ students speak out after becoming a political target

Amid hundreds of anti-LGBTQ+ bills nationwide, queer and trans youth are tired of waiting to be heard.

Shutterstock
Christopher WigginsAug 27, 2026

School has long been where American culture wars are rehearsed before they play out everywhere else. This year, LGBTQ+ students are once again the rehearsal’s central subject — and, as usual, its least consulted party. Lawmakers are debating over queer kids in hundreds of bills, yet strikingly few adults have thought to ask what the students themselves make of it.

The scale is enormous. The ACLU is currently tracking roughly 500 anti-LGBTQ+ bills moving through state legislatures, and the Trans Legislation Tracker counts just over 800 bills nationwide aimed at trans and gender-nonconforming people in 2026, with proposed education and health care legislation making up about half of that total.
