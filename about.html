About the Play

About
the Play

Play It Straight is a contemporary drama set in a public high school where a classroom comment ignites a broader political and cultural conflict.

01 — Synopsis

What happens.

When drama teacher Ross sarcastically tells a disruptive student to "sit on my lap," the moment is reported by a parent and escalates into a formal administrative inquiry.

As the school attempts to protect itself legally, students — particularly LGBTQ students — find their safety and autonomy threatened. The arts classroom, once a place of creativity and refuge, becomes a battleground over what can be said, who can exist openly, and who controls the narrative.

The play examines the consequences of politicizing education and the human cost of institutional self-protection.

It asks what it means to stand up for yourself and others when doing so carries real consequences — and what a teacher owes to a student when the law says one thing and the kid's life says another.

02 — For Students

What it's really about.

Play It Straight takes place in a high school drama class where students are encouraged to be creative and honest. When a joke during rehearsal is misunderstood and reported, the situation quickly grows into something much bigger than anyone expected.

Students find themselves caught between parents, administrators, and school policy. For some — especially LGBTQ students — the conflict becomes deeply personal. The play asks what it means to stand up for yourself and others when doing so carries real consequences.

Themes

  • Identity and self-definition
  • Student agency and autonomy
  • LGBTQ+ visibility and belonging
  • Artistic expression and censorship
  • Parental authority and institutional pressure

04 — Content Advisory

Know before
you produce.

This play contains strong language, homophobic slurs, discussion of conversion therapy, political extremism themes, accusations of misconduct, and mature discussions of sexuality and identity.

Organizations are encouraged to engage thoughtfully with the play's themes. Production approval is handled directly and collaboratively to support organizations in presenting the work responsibly and successfully.
