April 2025
College Production Cast of 10

Sacramento City College

Sacramento, CA

A college production that leaned into the script's comedic beats while preserving the emotional weight of the final act. The student ensemble brought strong chemistry and the intimate staging drew audiences into the conflict.

"The students owned the stage from the first line. You could hear the audience holding their breath during the inquiry scene." Director
April 2025
Community Theater Premiere

R25 Theater Company

Staged performances · April 2025

The R25 company was the first community theater production. A 4-week run with an ever-growing audience that netted the R25 group over $5,000 in profits and sparked the interest for two subsequent productions.

"It sparked exactly the kind of conversation our community needed." Audience Member
