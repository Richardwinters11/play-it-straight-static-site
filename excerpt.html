Act One — Opening
Lights up to reveal ROSS, a high school Drama teacher, standing in front of what appear to be 3 dead students. JENNY lies in state, on a funeral bier; IVAN is sprawled downstage of her, his face gory with blood which has gushed from his mouth; HOPE lies nearby, a cup in her hand, a dagger and an open binder just downstage of her head.
ROSS
Most important thing about teaching? Control the classroom. If you can't do that, nothing else you do is gonna matter.
(gestures at the corpses)
This, right here? First time I've felt in control since last Tuesday. It won't last, but can we just take a moment to enjoy it together?
(deep, meditative breath)
This is nice.
